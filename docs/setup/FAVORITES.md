# Channels and favorites

Open `/app/#/channels` and paste a channel slug or link from the same MarkeTOB server.
Public published pages can be previewed without signing in. A private page shows a
generic unavailable screen: sign in with an allowed account or ask its publisher.
No automatic access-request message is sent to a bot.

Choose **Add to favorites** on a readable channel. If signed out, **Sign in to save**
returns to that channel after login or account creation. Saving is explicit.
Favorites appear in the sidebar and in Channels on every session of the same account.

In Channels, search by title and use **Move up / Move down** for your saved order.
The visible search does not change that order. **Remove** deletes only the favorite.
The pilot allows 100 favorites. **New** means channel data or published layout has
advanced since your last view; opening the channel marks the rendered revision.

Use **Refresh** for remote changes during this stage. If access has been revoked or
the publisher has deleted the channel, only **Channel unavailable** remains, with
no cached title/content. You can remove the reference. Offline/hidden pages do not
retain source content; reopening verifies rights. Background updates arrive in PM-018.
